About Argentum

A different kind
of charter experience.

Argentum Yachting creates privately tailored yacht journeys along the Turkish Aegean and Mediterranean. From selecting the right yacht to shaping the route, onboard experience and every detail in between, each charter is planned around the people on board.

Built on more than 15 years of family experience in yachting, our approach combines local knowledge with personal guidance before and throughout your journey.

Not merely a charter, but a quieter way to experience the sea.
